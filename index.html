How a count day runs

  1. Before opening or after close, export stock-on-hand from GAAP and import it on the Items tab.
  2. Press New stocktake. Give each counter a location (kitchen, store room shelves, store room fridges, shop, front counter, bar). They count on their phone, or on a printed location sheet and capture later.
  3. If something is stored in more than one place, count it in each place. The app adds the locations into one total for GAAP.
  4. Keep Show GAAP qty off while counting, so people count what is there rather than what they expect.
  5. Check the Variance tab. Recount anything with a large value variance before you trust it.
  6. Need to stop half-way or start again? Press Save draft to keep a copy, and Clear all counts to empty the sheet. Restore any draft from the Drafts list.
  7. Finalise & lock, download the Excel file, then post the adjustments in GAAP.
